Agents for seismologyUW ESS
Problem

World-class physics, gated by expertise and glue code

  • Five workflow chains carry the field's output (catalogs, noise, sources, FWI, rupture). Healthy at the nodes, brittle at the joints — every group maintains private, unversioned converter scripts.
  • The citation-standard relocator (HypoDD) has had no maintained modern interface for a decade.
  • 3D simulation entry costs weeks: meshing, Par_file archaeology, HPC job wrangling. Errors surface as cryptic MPI crashes.
  • Keystone tools are one retirement or one dependency bump from breaking: CPS, NonLinLoc, disba, BayHunter.

A typical catalog pipeline today

FDSN / S3→ SeisBench pick→ PyOcto→ text-file glue→ NonLinLoc→ more glue→ HypoDD

Red joints = hand-written, untested, per-lab scripts. This is where science breaks and where novices lose weeks.

The consequence

Analysis capacity is bounded by scarce trained people, not by data or compute. Networks, regulators, and operators all report the same bottleneck: analysts.

Ecosystem

Hub-and-spoke observation, barbell modeling

Observation: three hubs

ObsPy (data objects, 30+ formats) · Pyrocko (GFZ; GF stores, Squirrel) · SeisBench (one API over all ML pickers + benchmark data).

Modeling: a barbell

One end: pip-install source inversion (Syngine → MTUQ, minutes on a laptop). Other end: HPC monoliths (SPECFEM3D, SeisSol). The middle — auto-config, meshing, orchestration — is thin, and it is exactly what Salvus (closed source) monetizes.

What that means for us

~70% of the stack wraps as agent tools at near zero cost (clean Python APIs). The valuable 30% is ~8 legacy text formats + config generation — bounded, testable work.

Open data is already cloud-resident

1.3 PBEarthScope + SCEDC + NCEDC continuous waveforms on AWS · 47,354 stations · anonymous or token access

Ground truth on demand

Precomputed Green's functions (Syngine REST, Pyrocko Greens Mill) give exact synthetic seismograms with zero simulation infrastructure — the eval generator is already deployed.

Prior art

The window: two half-systems, zero public benchmarks

TRACE — observation only

Shanghai AI Lab + USTC + Harvard (Mar 2026). Multi-agent: raw waveforms → 90k-event relocated catalogs → mechanistic interpretation (Ridgecrest, Santorini). 2,200+ analytical modules.

Benchmark is internal to one paper. Harness not released. No simulation branch.

SPECFEM-MCP — simulation only

Harbin/PKU (Dec 2025). First MCP server suite for SPECFEM 2D/3D/GLOBE: parameter generation → meshing → solver → visualization.

No evals shipped. Single group. Proves wrappers alone are replicable in months — not a moat.

What no one has

① An agent that closes the observation ↔ simulation loop (simulate a hypothesis, test it against data). ② A public, difficulty-graded benchmark for seismology agents — AstaBench, ScienceAgentBench, and every geo-agent benchmark contain zero waveform seismology. Both map directly onto Repère's existing design.

83 GW (2024) → ~238 GW by 2030 (GWEC). Capex ~$50–65B/yr → $100B+/yr. Every farm needs marine seismic surveys + geohazard assessment: site-investigation slice ≈ $1.5B/yr, growing ~11%/yr.

Data centers

Market · opening now

Three more doors: tailings dams, reinsurance, water & agriculture

Tailings dam monitoring

~8,500 facilities worldwide, only ~1,900 disclosed; lifetime failure rate ~1.2% — two orders of magnitude worse than water dams. Brumadinho cost Vale $7B; Samarco settled at ~$30B. GISTM (backed by investors holding >$25T AUM) mandates monitoring at every facility; only 67% of ICMM facilities conform. Insurance limits cut by a third, premiums doubled.

Our edge: dv/v ambient-noise monitoring sees inside the dam — internal erosion that InSAR, GNSS, and piezometers all miss (Olivier 2017 caught seepage other instruments didn't). IMS and Silixa already sell passive-seismic dam products; the analysis layer is the bottleneck.

Reinsurance & parametric

Six straight years of >$100B insured cat losses; $790B reinsurance capital; $65.6B cat bonds outstanding. Parametric insurance: ~$18B → ~$39B by 2030. ShakeMap is already de facto trigger infrastructure — and Liberty Mutual Re + Safehub launched ShakeNet Parametric (2024) marketing ground-truth shaking data as the basis-risk killer. Descartes Underwriting: >$200M GWP, 30%/yr. Proven willingness to pay for exactly what dense seismic analysis produces.

Water & agriculture (roadmap, not TAM)

Precision ag $11B→$24B; SGMA created 260+ groundwater agencies in California with >$500M in DWR grants (4× oversubscribed). Depth-resolved groundwater from ambient noise (Clements & Denolle 2018 → Mao 2025 Science) is our lab's own chain — TRL 3–4, no product yet, 3–7 yrs out. Honest position: a research thrust that the same agent + eval infrastructure de-risks.

Context

Seismology is a climate-risk sensor inside a larger geophysics stack

Three claims

  • Seismic sensing sees inside what every other sensor sees only the surface of. InSAR/GNSS measure surface deformation; dv/v measures interior state: dam seepage, depth-resolved groundwater, pre-failure landslide softening (Mainsant 2012: ~7% velocity drop days before failure). One method, three insured perils.
  • Climate-hazard seismology already works operationally in one place — Illgraben's ML debris-flow detector adds 20 min–1.5 hr of warning — and retrospectively everywhere else (GLOF tracking in Science 2018, landslide catalogs, flood discharge from river noise). The gap between "works in papers" and "runs as a service" is an agent-and-evals gap.
  • Seismic is the missing modality in the EO stack. NISAR ships 85 TB/day; DestinE has >€315M; Prithvi/Clay/Major TOM cover optical, SAR, climate — none ingest seismic waveforms. The seismic+EO fusion slot (and the multimodal foundation model) is uncontested.

Thesis

Agents are the demo. Evals are the moat.

  • Wrappers commoditize. SPECFEM-MCP shipped in months; frontier models write glue code better every quarter.
  • Trust does not commoditize. Regulators, DOE reviewers, and operators need measured missed-event rates, location error, and magnitude bias before they accept an autonomous result.
  • Whoever publishes the benchmark sets the standard others must beat — the LAB-Bench / FutureHouse position, unclaimed in seismology.
  • Repère already has the skeleton: InspectAI substrate, parametric truth sets, negative-case discipline, cost-Pareto leaderboard. This plan generalizes it from STA/LTA to the whole field.

Why synthetic evals

Ridgecrest, Tohoku, L'Aquila are in every LLM's training data — real-data evals measure memory. Our simulators produce waveforms with exact hidden truth (origin, mechanism, arrivals, structure), private seeds regenerate quarterly, and difficulty becomes continuous knobs: SNR, scene complexity, network geometry, model mismatch, tool tier, autonomy.

The modeling stack is the eval generator for the observational stack.

Plan

Three tiers, four phases

Tier 1 · Tools

Typed wrappers + MCP servers: cheap wraps of Python APIs (ObsPy, SeisBench, MTUQ, Instaseis…) plus codecs for the 8 legacy formats (NonLinLoc, HypoDD, GrowClust, Par_file…) with round-trip tests. Fixes the wrapper vacuum — a community contribution in itself.

Tier 2 · Operator agents

One per chain: DataScout · CatalogBuilder · SourceEstimator · NoiseMonitor · SimulationRunner · StructureInverter. Each self-verifies against deterministic oracles (cross-code GF agreement, travel-time sanity) before returning anything.

Tier 3 · Orchestrator

The scientist agent: plans a DAG across operators, routes steps by cost (FrugalRouter), and closes the observation↔simulation loop neither TRACE nor SPECFEM-MCP occupies.

Design rules

Frugality is architectural (small models for extraction/translation, frontier only for planning). Vendor and pin the fragile solo-maintainer layer. Provenance on every artifact — compliance-ready from day one. Human-in-the-loop is the product for regulated outputs.

The L0–L4 ladder

L0 one tool, clean data, full spec → L1 realistic noise, spec gaps → L2 multi-step chain, parameter choices scored → L3 model mismatch, negative cases, adversarial paths (judgment) → L4 underspecified scientific goal, orchestration scored.

Contamination policy

Public: generator code + worked seeds. Private: held-out seeds and perturbations, regenerated quarterly. Negative cases at fixed proportion from L1 up. Inspect-compatible logs; AstaBench-comparable metadata.

Model & funding

Nonprofit credibility, commercial capture, non-dilutive runway

Structure: the FutureHouse → Edison template

Academic/nonprofit arm builds agents, benchmarks, and publications (Schmidt-style AI-in-science philanthropy is funding exactly this). Commercial arm sells managed monitoring ($10–40k/mo/site), per-analysis compliance deliverables, and gempa-style open-core support. Edison spun out at $250M on this pattern in Dec 2025. Clean IP separation from day one.

Moats, restated

Published eval suites (the standard others must beat) · golden datasets + private synthetic generators · provenance-stamped compliance templates · community maintainership no closed vendor can buy.

Non-dilutive map

  • DOE — strongest fit: GTO/FORGE EGS FOAs, FECM storage, SBIR topics in geothermal MMV + CCS monitoring.
  • NSF — NAIRR (bipartisan, natural home for agentic geoscience CI), CSSI/Geoinformatics.
  • EU — DT-GEO follow-ons, ChEESE-2P, Destination Earth: funding the containerized plumbing agents sit on; hedges US volatility.
  • Philanthropy — Schmidt AI-in-science, Project InnerSpace's stated ~$6M AI gap.

Honest risks

Fragmented TAM (niches of $10s–100s of M each) · incumbents bundling LLMs onto existing contracts · liability in regulated outputs (human sign-off is the product) · federal funding volatility · best commercial data is proprietary.
